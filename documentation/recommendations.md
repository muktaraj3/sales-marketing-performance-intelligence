# Strategic Recommendations

The following recommendations are based on customer spending patterns, campaign responses, household characteristics and purchase behaviour identified in the analysis.

## 1. Prioritise High-Spending Product Categories

Wine and meat account for approximately 77.73% of total customer spending.

**Recommendation:**
- Prioritise these two product categories in product-focused marketing initiatives.
- Explore complementary product bundles.
- Monitor whether these categories continue to represent the largest share of customer spending.

## 2. Focus on High-Value Customer Segments

Customers in the fourth spending quartile account for approximately 61.53% of total spending.

**Recommendation:**
- Develop retention initiatives for high-spending customers.
- Explore loyalty benefits and personalised product recommendations.
- Monitor changes in spending among high-value customers.

## 3. Improve Campaign Engagement

The latest campaign response rate is higher among customers with shorter purchase recency.

**Recommendation:**
- Prioritise timely engagement with recently active customers.
- Test different campaign messages for less recently active customers.
- Monitor response rates across recency groups.

## 4. Tailor Campaigns to Household Composition

Customers without children show a higher latest campaign response rate than customers in the other household groups.

**Recommendation:**
- Explore household-based customer segmentation.
- Test relevant product messaging across household groups.
- Avoid assuming that household composition alone determines campaign response.

## 5. Review Income-Based Customer Segments

Average customer spending increases across the analysed income segments.

**Recommendation:**
- Explore differentiated product recommendations across income segments.
- Use income alongside spending behaviour rather than as the sole targeting criterion.
- Exclude missing and flagged income records from income-based comparisons until reviewed.

## 6. Consider Geographical Differences

Spain and Saudi Arabia show relatively high latest campaign response rates among the larger country groups.

**Recommendation:**
- Examine campaign engagement patterns by country.
- Explore region-specific messaging and campaign approaches.
- Consider sample sizes when comparing countries, particularly Mexico, which has only three customers.

## 7. Evaluate Previous Campaign Performance

Campaign 4 has the highest acceptance rate among the five previous campaigns, while Campaign 2 has the lowest.

**Recommendation:**
- Review the messaging and audience characteristics associated with Campaign 4.
- Investigate the lower acceptance observed for Campaign 2.
- Use these findings to inform future campaign testing.

## 8. Strengthen High-Value Customer Engagement

The fourth spending quartile has a latest campaign response rate of 30%, compared with 5.54% for the first quartile.

**Recommendation:**
- Consider targeted engagement strategies for high-value customers.
- Monitor retention and repeat engagement among this segment.
- Evaluate whether similar approaches improve engagement in other customer segments.

## 9. Understand Purchase Behaviour Across Channels

Store purchases have the highest average purchase count, followed by web and catalogue purchases.

**Recommendation:**
- Continue monitoring customer purchase activity across store, web and catalogue channels.
- Explore ways to improve engagement across lower-frequency purchase channels.
- Avoid treating purchase counts as channel revenue or marketing attribution.

## Analytical Considerations

- The dataset contains customer-level aggregated spending rather than transaction-level sales.
- Actual marketing expenditure is unavailable, so marketing ROI cannot be calculated.
- Campaign acceptance patterns indicate association, not causation.
- Country comparisons should account for differences in customer sample sizes.
